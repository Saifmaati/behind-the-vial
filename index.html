What actually happens inside the body after injecting a peptide that is trending online, and what the evidence really says.

Scroll to follow one injection

  1. 01 · The vial

    Sold online as a “research chemical”

    Not approved for use in people. Nothing on the label can tell you what, or how much, is really inside.

  2. 02 · The syringe

    One thin needle, one drop

    This film never shows how to mix, measure or inject. It follows what happens after.

  3. 03 · The site

    Into the skin of the belly

    Shots like this go under the skin of the belly, the thigh or the upper arm.

  4. 04 · Under the skin

    A small pool forms in the fat

    The drug sits in the fat layer as a depot, and seeps out slowly.

  5. 05 · Into the blood

    Through the walls of tiny blood vessels

    It slips into capillaries, and the blood carries it on toward the heart.

  6. 06 · Through the body

    It acts where its receptors are

    The blood takes it around the whole body. The organs that carry its receptors respond.
