# Data wording: decisions

Scope: `data/retatrutide/*.js`, `data/graymarket.js`, `data/peptides.js`, `data/riskitems.js`. Rule from the owner: never show prices, buy or shop language, discount codes, seller names or vendor links. One line each: the decision, then why.

1. **Purchase verbs are replaced with neutral ones: "sold online", "obtained", "people using vials sold as retatrutide", "injected retatrutide they got on their own".** Meaning, qualifiers, numbers, sources and ledger ids stay the same.
2. **"a doctor who had ordered peptides from China" became "had obtained peptides from China".** "Ordered" is purchase language. The charge, the date and the country are unchanged.
3. **"Vials are sent in by sellers and buyers" became "Vials come from sellers and from people who mail them in for testing".** This follows the ledger notes on `gray-finnrick-scale`: samples come from vendors and from mail-in testing.
4. **"optional paid extra tests" became "optional add-on tests".** The point is that sterility and endotoxin tests are not part of the standard panel. "Paid" pointed readers at a commercial service.
5. **"after these drugs went on sale" became "after these drugs were approved" (3 places).** The label's term is postmarketing, which means after approval. The new wording keeps that meaning and avoids sales wording.
6. **The two Finnrick certificate examples (both 99.9% pure, one 76% under its label, one 45% over) are no longer cited.** Those certificate pages and their URLs name the seller of each vial, so citing them puts a vendor link and a seller name in #sources. The −76% to +45% range and the 98 of 163 figure still come from the main Finnrick page. The card now ends: "even a vial that holds the right drug can hold much less or much more than its label says".
7. **FDA warning letters whose titles name a seller are no longer cited (Peptide Partners LLC; Lovega LLC dba Pink Pony Peptides; GenLabMeds).** #sources prints each cited title, and two of those names are on the commerce test's seller list.
   - The "5 more letters in August 2026" fact is now traced to the FDA's own warning-letter index (`gray-fda-letters-21-total`), which carries the same count and date.
   - The "six online and telehealth businesses selling compounded retatrutide" sentence was dropped, because no other source supports it. The index still gives "6 in September 2025" in the first enforcement card.
   - The "Injecting skips some of the body's defenses" contamination card was dropped for the same reason. The TGA sterility card still covers infection risk.
   - Claim 7 ("natural, so gentle") lost the same FDA sentence. Two already-verified facts replace it: its own half-life claim ("effects, including side effects, do not wear off quickly") and the TRIUMPH-1 nausea rate (`claims-3-gi-rates-triumph1`).
8. **Kept: "sellers", "sold online", "selling", "not stopping sales" and "influencers as sales partners".** These words describe the illegal market critically. They are not commerce from this site, and the brief's gray-market section cannot be written without them.
9. **Kept: the Victoria alert's product labels "Retatrutide, Reta, R-10 or R-20".** A state health alert uses these labels so that people can recognize the affected products. They are not seller names.
10. **Kept: brand names of approved medicines (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Egrifta, Scenesse, Vyleesi, Forzinity, Zadaxin).** They name regulator-approved products, not sellers, and readers need them to recognize the drugs.
11. **Vial labels "Australian study, sample 1/2/3" became "vial 1/2/3", keeping the paper's own numbering.** The owner bans the word "Sample" on screen, and these are vials.
12. **Removed the `chart: { columns }` hints from the dose-fact trials.** The owner banned graphs site-wide and `js/ui/dosefacts.js` now renders tables only, so the hints were dead data. They were optional in the contract.
13. **The old working title was removed from the header comments of `core.js` and `side-effects.js`.** Required by `tests/name.test.mjs`.

## Not in my files (for the owners of those files)

None of them is cited any more, so none is rendered, but the file is public on GitHub Pages. Suggestion: emit only sources cited by `data/`.
- Three cited source titles still read commercially in #sources:
  - `finnrick-2026-retatrutide-safety-testing-results`: "Retatrutide Safety Testing Results & Vendor Ratings". The page itself lists vendors with prices, but it is the main independent vial-testing source (DECISIONS.md 7).
  - `therapeutic-goods-2026-peptide-products-topic-page`: "… / Risks of buying peptide products online".
  - Suggestion: shorten the displayed titles in the ledger, for example to "Retatrutide safety testing results" and "Peptide products (topic page)".
